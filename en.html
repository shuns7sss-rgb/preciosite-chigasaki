FAQ

How does check-in work?

This is an unstaffed self check-in property. After booking, please submit the guest registration through the pre-check-in link we send you. Once it is done, we send you the entry instructions and key information by message. Key information is not sent until pre-check-in is complete.

Can I arrive late at night?

Yes. With self check-in you can enter at any time after the check-in time. Please be quiet when entering at night out of consideration for the neighbours.

Is there parking?

No. Please use a nearby coin parking lot.

Which floor is it on? Is there an elevator?

The apartment is on the 2nd floor. There is no elevator and no balcony. The ground floor is a restaurant.

Are towels and detergent provided?

One bath towel and one face towel per guest are provided (not replaced during your stay). Laundry detergent is not provided.

Is smoking or partying allowed?

No smoking indoors. This is not a party venue. If neighbours complain about noise, you may be charged for the cost of handling it.

Can I extend my stay?

Please contact us by 18:00 on the day before check-out. After 18:00 the day before, half of the cleaning fee is added. Same-day extensions on the check-out day are not accepted.

Do I need to show ID?

Japanese law requires us to register every guest. Guests of Japanese nationality do not need to enter a passport image or number.
